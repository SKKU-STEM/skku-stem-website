title: Untangling copper's two-stage electromigration with in situ 4D‑STEM
summary: "In copper interconnects, electromigration drives atoms to drift under current until the line fails, and its mechanism is hard to resolve because several diffusion pathways compete at once. Using in situ four-dimensional scanning transmission electron microscopy (4D‑STEM), we mapped orientation, structure, and mass loss in real time, untangling a clear two-stage mechanism: atoms move first along grain boundaries, then faster across a void's surface."
